How to Play Tapix

1. Tap: Tap the green face (or press Space on a laptop) to earn Coins — starting at 1 Coin per tap.
2. Upgrade: Press any green Buy button! Power Tap (100 Coins) boosts you from 1 to 10 Coins per tap, scaling all the way up to 15M Coins.
3. Golden Stars: Tap glowing Golden Stars within 10 seconds for a Coin jackpot or 2x Overclock surge.
4. Rebirth: At 100,000 run Coins, Rebirth for permanent +50% Click Power, +50% Faster Coins/Sec, and Prestige Gems!
