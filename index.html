How to Play Tapix

1. Tap: Tap the Tapix Core (or press Space on a laptop) to earn Coins — starting at 1 Coin per tap.
2. Upgrade: Press any green Buy button! Buying Power Tap boosts you from 1 to 10 Coins per tap, and each upgrade adds a different boost.
3. Golden Stars: Tap glowing Golden Stars within 10 seconds for a Coin jackpot or 2x Overclock surge.
4. Rebirth: At 100,000 run Coins, Rebirth for permanent Prestige Gems (+25% Coins each).
